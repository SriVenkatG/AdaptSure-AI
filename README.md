🚀 AdaptSure AI – Adaptive Insurance for Gig Workers

## 📌 Problem

Delivery workers (Swiggy, Zomato, Amazon, etc.) depend on daily earnings. However, due to real-world disruptions like heavy rain, extreme heat, pollution, or sudden curfews, they are often unable to work and lose income.

At present, there is no reliable system that protects their earnings during these situations. Most existing solutions do not verify whether the worker was actually affected or not.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To build an intelligent insurance platform that:
- Protects gig workers from income loss
- Detects real-world disruptions in real time
- Verifies actual work impact before payout
- Automatically compensates workers fairly
- Prevents misuse and false claims

---

## 👤 Target User (Persona)

Food delivery partners (Swiggy / Zomato) working in urban areas.

Example:

Ravi is a delivery partner working in Hyderabad. During heavy rainfall, deliveries in his area drop significantly, and he is unable to earn for several hours. Our system detects this situation and compensates him automatically.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

We propose an **Adaptive AI-driven Parametric Insurance System** that goes beyond simple weather-based triggers.

Instead of blindly paying based on weather conditions, the system verifies **real income disruption** using multiple signals.

The platform:
- Monitors environmental conditions
- Tracks delivery activity trends (simulated)
- Verifies whether the worker was actually impacted
- Calculates real income loss
- Triggers automatic payouts

---

## ⚙️ System Workflow

1. Worker registers on the platform
2. System calculates a weekly premium based on risk profile
3. Worker activates insurance coverage
4. System continuously monitors:
   - Weather conditions
   - Location data
   - Delivery activity trends (mock data)
5. Disruption detected (e.g., heavy rain)
6. System verifies:
   - Worker inactivity
   - Drop in delivery demand in that zone
7. Income loss is estimated
8. Claim is automatically generated
9. Payout is processed instantly

---

## 💰 Weekly Pricing Model

Premium is calculated weekly based on:

- Average earnings of the worker
- Historical risk of disruptions in that area
- Frequency of past events

Example:

- Low-risk zone → ₹40/week  
- Medium-risk zone → ₹60/week  
- High-risk zone → ₹80/week  

This ensures fair and adaptive pricing.

---

## ⚡ Parametric Triggers

The system automatically triggers claims when:

- Rainfall > 40mm  
- Temperature > 45°C  
- AQI > 300  
- Curfew / zone restriction  
- Significant drop in delivery activity  

---

## 🤖 AI / ML Integration

AI is used to make the system intelligent and reliable:

- Risk Prediction  
  → Predict disruption probability based on historical data  

- Dynamic Premium Calculation  
  → Adjust pricing based on risk  

- Fraud Detection  
  → Detect abnormal claims using:
    - GPS inconsistencies  
    - unusual inactivity patterns  
    - duplicate claim behavior  

- Disruption Prediction  
  → Forecast events before they occur  

---

## 🛡️ Unique Feature: Real Impact Verification

Unlike basic solutions, this system verifies actual impact before payout.

A claim is approved only when:

- Disruption is confirmed  
- Worker is unable to perform deliveries  
- Delivery demand drops in that zone  

This ensures fairness and prevents false claims.
